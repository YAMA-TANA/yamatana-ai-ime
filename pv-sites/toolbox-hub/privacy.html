PRIVACY POLICY · UPDATED 2026-09-12

Privacy, plainly.

This policy applies to TOOLBOX HUB and the browser tools linked from it. It is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

Files and local processing

PDFs, images, audio, fonts, SQLite files and STL files are processed in your browser by the tools that support them. Those tools are designed not to upload the selected file to our server. You remain responsible for the files you choose to open.

Analytics

The sites include Google Analytics with measurement ID G-YZ8R7GTWQR to understand general page usage. Google may process technical information such as page URL, device and approximate region according to its own policies. You can block analytics with browser privacy controls.

Third-party requests

Some features call a third-party service only when you use them. For example, GIS place search requests the search phrase to a geocoding API, and map tiles are served by OpenStreetMap tile infrastructure. External CDN libraries may also receive a request when a page loads. Their own privacy policies apply.

Advertising links

Pages may contain a clearly marked sponsored recommendation link. Following it takes you to a third-party website. We do not control that website or its data practices.

Storage and contact

These sites do not require an account and do not intentionally store the files you process. Browser settings such as localStorage may remember language choices. If you want a particular page removed or corrected, use the project repository issue tracker linked from the site directory.

Changes

We may update this policy when features or providers change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
