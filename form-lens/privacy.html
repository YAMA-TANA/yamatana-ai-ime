Privacy, plainly.

最終更新: 2026-09-12 · This page is a plain-language summary and is not legal advice.

Camera and pose processing

Camera frames are processed in your browser by MediaPipe Tasks Vision. FORM LENS does not record or upload the camera video. Only calculated landmarks and session summaries are used to update the interface.

Workout logs

Session history is stored in your browser. If you use the exercise atlas, the app may request exercise metadata from the FORM LENS Workers API, which can cache public wger data. Session summary and optional quality data may be sent to the FORM LENS API for aggregate insights when you finish a session.

Location and weather

The weather feature requests location only after you choose it. Coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo through the API request needed to return current weather. You can use the camera studio without granting location.

Analytics and advertising

Google Analytics measurement ID G-YZ8R7GTWQR is included to understand general page usage. The page also contains a clearly marked sponsored external link. Third-party providers apply their own policies.

Language and changes

Your language choice may be saved in localStorage. We may update this policy when the app, API or providers change. The date above identifies the current revision.
